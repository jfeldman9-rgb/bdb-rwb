## 1. Score: 5 / 10 for game feel + pacing

BDB is now long enough and clearable enough to judge as a game rather than a snack — the length rebuild genuinely landed (Adventure bot 153–219 s per stage, ~9.2 min campaign, inside the RWB Stage-1 band of 154–216 s) — and the skeleton underneath is sound: poise-gated flinch, staggered flanking waves, real 3-phase bosses with roar beats, a Dad call-in whose wipe is timed to the swat impact frame, and a Papacito finisher with an explicit anti-softlock drip. What holds the score at 5 is that the top layer, the thing a player actually touches 5 times a second, is essentially unbuilt: the entire RWB feel rig in [fx.js](../src/fx.js) (tiered hit-stop, trauma shake, sparks, dust, hit-lights, slow-mo) is dead code that nothing imports, so the core verb "hold FIRE" produces a flat 4-frame freeze, no recoil, no muzzle flash, no splat, and enemies that delete in one frame instead of dying. Pacing reaches RWB minutes by HP inflation and metronomic wave repetition rather than by varied set-pieces, and Nightmare — the reward for beating the game — is admittedly not bot-clearable. Structure: 7/10. Moment-to-moment touch: 3/10. Blended feel + pacing verdict: **5/10**.

## 2. Findings (ranked)

### Critical

#### F1 — The whole feel rig is dead code: `FX` is never imported, instantiated, or ticked
- **Where:** [src/fx.js:3](../src/fx.js:3) `export class FX`; no importer anywhere in `src/` or `tests/` (verified by search: the only `fx` hits are the file itself, `PapacitoFX`, and `sfx`). [src/game-scene.js:53](../src/game-scene.js:53) `create()` builds backdrop/HUD/PapiFX but never `new FX(this)`. [src/quality-governor.js:80](../src/quality-governor.js:80) still writes `st.fx.quality`, i.e. it assumes a `scene.fx` that the gameplay scene never sets — and the governor itself is never installed from [src/main.js](../src/main.js).
- **Player impact:** None of the designed juice can ever appear: tiered hit-stop + `slowmo` ([fx.js:40](../src/fx.js:40)), trauma shake ([fx.js:67](../src/fx.js:67)), directional sparks, dust/snow puffs, embers, debris, impact flash, dynamic hit-light. The game the player feels is the fallback `Graphics` ellipses + 5/15-dot bursts in [game-scene.js:88](../src/game-scene.js:88). This is the single highest-leverage feel fact in the repo.
- **Evidence:** `HITSTOP = { light: 4, medium: 6, heavy: 9, finisher: 14 }` and `SHAKE = { light: 0.16, … finisher: 0.75 }` in [src/config.js:10](../src/config.js:10) are only consumed inside the dead class. [BUILD-PLAN.md](../plan/BUILD-PLAN.md) §6.5 promised "Eggplant splat, hit-stop `HITSTOP.light`, feather burst" as the hit-feel polish step; it has not happened.
- **Concrete fix:** Instantiate `FX` once in `GameScene.create()`, tick `fx.update(dt)` + apply `fx.shakeOffset()` to camera scroll in `syncViews()`, and route `hit/ko/hurt/shield/dadhit` events to `fx.impact(kind,…)` / `fx.thump(…)` / `fx.boom(…)`. Then either wire `installGovernor` or delete its `st.fx` assumption — do not leave a third, half-believed quality path. Keep [fx.js](../src/fx.js) textures/particles as-is for the first pass; the ask is wiring, not new art.

#### F2 — Hit-stop is flat (4 frames for everything) and, at 5 shots/s, it becomes constant judder that also eats inputs
- **Where:** [src/campaign.js:122](../src/campaign.js:122) `if (result.accepted) { this.hitStop = 4/60; … }` — identical for chip hit, bonk, shield-follow-up hit, KO, and boss phase-gate hit. Freeze is consumed at [campaign.js:187](../src/campaign.js:187) by returning before Bob/enemies/projectiles move. In the browser loop, [src/game-scene.js:127](../src/game-scene.js:127) additionally substitutes `{}` for live input whenever `w.hitStop > 0`, so buffered presses during the freeze are discarded (`inp.snapshot()` is never called that step; `Input.take()` windows in [src/input.js](../src/input.js) expire unobserved).
- **Player impact:** Holding FIRE into a pack (the dominant strategy) freezes ~67 ms per connecting shot at a 200 ms fire interval — roughly a third of wall-clock frozen while connecting. Because every outcome freezes the same amount, a KO feels like a graze and a boss gate feels like a walker tap. On a phone this reads as hitching / dropped frames, not weight. The input swallow makes jump/dash presses during a stream intermittently unresponsive in exactly the moments the player is trying to dodge.
- **Evidence:** [src/eggplant.js:1](../src/eggplant.js:1) `interval: .2` (5/s) × flat `4/60` s. Contrast the unused tier table in [config.js:10](../src/config.js:10) and `FX.impact()` slow-mo for `heavy/finisher` in [fx.js:48](../src/fx.js:48). This is P1 item 1 in [MUSE-100-IMPROVEMENTS.md](MUSE-100-IMPROVEMENTS.md) and it is still open verbatim.
- **Concrete fix:** Tier it in `Campaign.hit()`: chip `light` (or 0–2 frames for non-flinch poise hits), bonk/shield-break `medium`, KO `heavy`, boss-gate/finisher `finisher` + brief slow-mo. Cap back-to-back freeze (e.g. `Math.max`, never additive — already the case in dead `FX`, port that semantics). Stop discarding input during hit-stop: snapshot every step and let `Bob` buffer (`jumpBuffer`/`dash` already exist) instead of passing `{}`.

#### F3 — Nightmare is shipped as the clear reward but is not clearable (S2/S3 Zone-5 snowball wall)
- **Where:** [docs/LENGTH-DIFFICULTY.md](LENGTH-DIFFICULTY.md) "Known gaps" (explicit: S2/S3 zone 5 wave 2 snowball wall; also not clearable before the rebuild). Composition at fault: [src/stage2-def.js:20](../src/stage2-def.js:20) Zone 5 and [src/stage3-def.js:20](../src/stage3-def.js:20) Zone 5 stack 2–3 simultaneous ranged attackers (guard/hover/choir) plus a charger/lecturer with no cap on in-flight hostiles. Attack cadence compounds: Nightmare `pace 1.22` × chapter ramp capped only at `PACE_CAP 1.25` ([src/campaign.js:18](../src/campaign.js:18)), `recovery .85`, damage dealt `×.8` ([src/difficulty.js:4](../src/difficulty.js:4)), choir buff `×1.28` cooldown tick ([src/yettis.js:68](../src/yettis.js:68)).
- **Player impact:** The one difficulty a finisher unlocks is the one that walls. Because every hostile hit costs a full heart (see F19) and Bob has 3 hearts with only 0.55 s of post-hit invulnerability, overlapping same-lane snowballs/halos delete a run without a readable mistake. "Does not trivially win" has tipped into "does not win."
- **Evidence:** Stage 5 waves carry 4–6 enemies with delays ≤3.4 s; ranged reach is 580 px with a ±110 px lane tolerance ([yettis.js:120](../src/yettis.js:120)), so guards/hovers start firing from off the player's immediate focus. No test pins Nightmare (the length floors in [LENGTH-DIFFICULTY.md](LENGTH-DIFFICULTY.md) pin Adventure only).
- **Concrete fix:** Thin Zone-5 Wave-2 ranged overlap (stagger so ≤2 hostiles are in flight per lane; convert one ranged slot per wave to walker/charger), and add a Nightmare bot-clear floor to the length test before calling Nightmare tuned. Do not fix this by lowering global pace — Adventure/Story pacing is already in band.

### High

#### F4 — Firing the joke weapon is visually silent: no recoil, no muzzle flash, no splat
- **Where:** [src/campaign.js:206](../src/campaign.js:206) emits a position-less `'fire'`; [src/game-scene.js:112](../src/game-scene.js:112) handles it with audio only (`sfx.fire()`). [src/eggplant.js:2](../src/eggplant.js:2) `createShot()` knows the muzzle (`bob.x + facing*58`, `y - z - 150`) but that point is never sent to the view. In-flight shots and impacts are procedural ellipses in [game-scene.js:104](../src/game-scene.js:104); on hit, `stepShot()` just sets `alive = false` ([eggplant.js:18](../src/eggplant.js:18)).
- **Player impact:** The eggplant is the title joke ("I brought the eggplant.") and the primary verb, yet firing produces no kick on Bob (beyond the `shoot` anim from `shootTime = .22`, [eggplant.js:9](../src/eggplant.js:9)), no flash at the hand, no spin/squash, and a vanishing purple pill on impact. At 5/s this is the difference between a toy gun and a stream of UI dots. `sfx.fire()` itself ([src/audio.js:157](../src/audio.js:157)) is a slow 0.25 s band-pass sweep with 50 ms attack — softer/longer than one shot interval, so even the audio smears instead of popping per shot.
- **Evidence:** No `recoil/muzzle/splat` symbol exists in `src/`. Bonks (<90 px, [eggplant.js:10](../src/eggplant.js:10)) share the identical `hit` event with only a `bonk` boolean that the view ignores ([game-scene.js:121](../src/game-scene.js:121)).
- **Concrete fix:** Emit `{x, y, dir}` on `fire`; draw a 1–2 frame muzzle flash + 2 px opposite-facing view nudge + 2–3 purple chips; give the shot a 2-frame spin/squash and a short splat burst/decal (capped count) at the `hit` point. Shorten `sfx.fire` attack and gate it per shot. Give the bonk its own thump/ring — it is currently undiscoverable as a distinct mechanic.

#### F5 — Enemies do not die on screen; they are deleted in one frame
- **Where:** [src/game-scene.js:71](../src/game-scene.js:71) builds views only for `[bob, ...enemies.filter(e => e.alive)]` and [game-scene.js:80](../src/game-scene.js:80) destroys any view not in that live set the same frame. Death in sim is instant: [src/yettis.js:57](../src/yettis.js:57) `alive = false; state = 'dead'`; [src/papacito.js:48](../src/papacito.js:48) and [src/dad-call.js:41](../src/dad-call.js:41) do the same for wipes.
- **Player impact:** The `knockdown` mapping in [game-scene.js:21](../src/game-scene.js:21) (`dead → knockdown`) and the fading-corpse shadow logic in [src/fighter.js:41](../src/fighter.js:41) are unreachable for enemies — the sprite is gone before either can play. KOs read as pop-outs with 15 beige dots at `y-100` ([game-scene.js:121](../src/game-scene.js:121)), regardless of whether an eggplant, the Papacito crowd, or Dad's swatter did it. Boss adds and phase-transition corpses vanish identically.
- **Evidence:** [BUILD-PLAN.md](../plan/BUILD-PLAN.md) §4.2 promised feather-burst deaths and heart/feather Papacito deaths; `recordKo()` already records `source` (`bob/papi/dad`, [campaign.js:108](../src/campaign.js:108)) but the view collapses `hit` and `ko` into one branch that ignores it.
- **Concrete fix:** Keep dead enemies view-alive for one knockdown/fade beat (~0.45 s, non-colliding) before destroy; branch burst color/shape by `source` and scale count by `boss`. Bob's own death (`dead` 1.1 s → respawn, [src/bob.js:19](../src/bob.js:19)) already proves the pattern — extend it to Yettis.

#### F6 — Getting hurt has almost no body: no knockback on Bob, no trauma shake, no vignette — only a sound and a blink
- **Where:** [src/bob.js:13](../src/bob.js:13) `takeHit()` changes hp/state/invulnerability but never displaces Bob (`x` is untouched in every branch). [src/game-scene.js:112](../src/game-scene.js:112) handles `hurt` with `sfx.hurt()` only. The only camera shakes in the entire view are Dad's hit ([game-scene.js:119](../src/game-scene.js:119) `shake(160, .008)`) and a flash on `wingman_phase3` ([game-scene.js:111](../src/game-scene.js:111)). `Fighter.shudder` ([src/fighter.js:8](../src/fighter.js:8)) is initialized and rendered ([fighter.js:39](../src/fighter.js:39)) but never set above 0.
- **Player impact:** Enemy knockback exists and is well-tuned (12 px chip / 65 px bonk, poise-gated, [yettis.js:59](../src/yettis.js:59)) but the return direction is empty: a charger at 430 px/s, a snowball, and a walker slap all stop Bob in place with the same sound. Losing one of three hearts mid-pack on a bright phone screen is easy to miss; the knockdown→getup state machine (0.65 s → 0.4 s, 1.9 s i-frames, [bob.js:49](../src/bob.js:49)) is good rules with no punctuation.
- **Evidence:** Trauma/shake/slow-mo all live in dead [fx.js](../src/fx.js). Live `sfx` calls in the scene are exactly: `fire, pickup, hurt, clang, levelClear, papi, dadHorn, hit` — no `thud/shaken/impact` on Bob damage despite those recipes existing in [src/audio.js:152](../src/audio.js:152).
- **Concrete fix:** Add a small facing-away slide on `hurt` and a larger one on `knockdown` (sim-side, clamped to bounds), fire `fx.impact('heavy')`-class shake + red edge pulse on `hurt`, and set `shudder` for 2–3 frames. Keep the existing invulnerability timings — they are fair; they just need to be visible as more than alpha blinking at 12 Hz ([game-scene.js:78](../src/game-scene.js:78)).

#### F7 — RWB minutes were bought with repetition: 15 same-shaped waves per stage and HP-sponge scaling, with empty walk-ups and no mid-stage set-pieces
- **Where:** Every stage is 5 zones × 3 waves + boss over 7760 px ([src/config.js:6](../src/config.js:6)); 53 / 55 / 56 wave enemies (counted from [stage1-def.js](../src/stage1-def.js), [stage2-def.js](../src/stage2-def.js), [stage3-def.js](../src/stage3-def.js)); `hpScale: 2.2` on all three stages lifts the non-boss HP pool to 4182 / 4735 / 5000 (vs RWB Stage-1's 492 at RWB damage scale — different scale, same lesson: duration via pool). The only per-zone flags in the whole campaign are `shakes: true` (S1 Zone 2) and `dad: true` (S1 Zone 3). Between arenas there is an 80 px walk-up with nothing in it; [src/campaign.js:226](../src/campaign.js:226) unlock → banner "CHECKPOINT: EGO INTACT" → walk → next lock, 15 times per stage. Chapter change is a 2.5 s frozen `clearTimer` ([campaign.js:184](../src/campaign.js:184)) then a hard `scene.restart` with the boot overlay ([game-scene.js:129](../src/game-scene.js:129)).
- **Player impact:** Bot time says "RWB-length"; a human feels a metronome: lock, 3–5 staggered Yettis, KEEP GOING, repeat, with escalation expressed as "same pack, more HP and one more role" rather than new spatial or timing ideas. Zones 4–5 in every stage test earlier lessons but introduce nothing, which is where the padding sensation concentrates. There are no mid-stage cut beats after the S1 tutorials: `kidnap` fires once at Stage-2 load and `rescue_pre_boss` once at the Stage-3 boss ([campaign.js:44](../src/campaign.js:44), [campaign.js:95](../src/campaign.js:95)); everything between is the loop.
- **Evidence:** Later-zone mix is test-enforced (≥3 roles) but drawn from the same six roles every wave; the last S3 wave is 6 enemies over 4 s of delays ([stage3-def.js:23](../src/stage3-def.js:23)). Boss plates/minion patterns are the only true set-pieces, arriving after ~2.5 min of sameness per stage.
- **Concrete fix:** Do not add minutes. Re-shape inside the budget: make one Zone-4 wave per stage a breather/oddity (2 light enemies + shake/crate-style pickup moment), give Zones 4–5 one compositional twist each (e.g. double-charger crossfire already exists at S1Z5W1 — vary the others), and dress at least the S2/S3 walk-ups with the existing parallax/plate system plus a bark or caption beat. Re-verify each stage stays 140–240 s after any retune.

#### F8 — Every zone clear fully heals, so attrition never accumulates inside a 15-wave stage
- **Where:** [src/campaign.js:229](../src/campaign.js:229) zone-clear branch: `this.bob.hp = this.bob.maxHp` unconditionally (15 full heals per stage counting waves→zones; 5 zone heals + boss-checkpoint restarts).
- **Player impact:** Chip damage is cosmetic: survive the current arena in any state and the next starts at full hearts. Combined with "lose a life, keep progress" respawns (wave retention + boss-HP retention, [campaign.js:161](../src/campaign.js:161) — the right anti-frustration call for a deterministic sim), Adventure tension is purely within-arena. The bot's stage hit counts (S1 4 / S2 8 / S3 14 solo-stage hits, per [LENGTH-DIFFICULTY.md](LENGTH-DIFFICULTY.md)) never compound into a campaign health story.
- **Evidence:** No `zoneHeal` knob exists in [difficulty.js](../src/difficulty.js); Story (5 hearts) and Nightmare (3 hearts) heal identically.
- **Concrete fix:** Heal +1 heart on zone clear for Adventure/Nightmare; keep full heal for Story and at boss checkpoints. Any change here must re-run the bot bands — expect S2/S3 deaths to rise; pair it with F3 thinning, not as a standalone difficulty hike.

#### F13 — Bosses have good rules and flat presentation: roar without shake/slow-mo, pop-in adds, a teleporting dive, and a stationary Choirmaster

## 4. Stage pacing notes

Numbers are Adventure bot clears from [LENGTH-DIFFICULTY.md](LENGTH-DIFFICULTY.md) (30/60/120 Hz). Structure per stage: 5 zones × 3 waves (15 waves) + boss, 7760 px, `hpScale 2.2`. Wave-enemy counts counted from the defs.

### Stage 1 — BOULEVARD OF BETTER JUDGMENT (Interventionist, 1200 HP): **OK, with late padding**
- Bot: 153.6 / 159.3 / 156.1 s; solo-stage 4 hits / 0 deaths (60 Hz). Boss fight ~33.8 s. 53 wave enemies. The only stage whose length feels *earned*: Zones 1–3 teach in order (shield→dash, hover-lane + first rush with the guaranteed shake trio at Zone 2, choir-first + Dad unlock at Zone 3), each first-two-waves carrying a first-run tutorial.
- Zones 4–5 (lecturer/guard/hover/charger mixes; double-charger crossfire opener at Z5W1 is the stage's best late wave) test without teaching and are where a human will first feel the metronome. Verdict: **not too short anymore; last two zones are padding-flavored but acceptable as an exam.** Interventionist Phase III (arena charge + two-way slam) is a proper RWB-style climax; the fight is the shortest of the three bosses because Bob arrives with Dad + Papi lessons fresh — correct for Stage 1.

### Stage 2 — THE CELESTIAL INTERVENTION (Choirmaster, 1140 HP): **Padding-leaning, unfair tail on Nightmare**
- Bot: 186.8 / 193.7 / 190.9 s; 8 hits / 1 death. Boss fight ~52.0 s (longest). 55 wave enemies; chapter pace ×1.1. Opens at full mix (lecturer in Wave 1) with no new verb, so the first minute reads as "Stage 1, again, bluer." Distinct beats exist — guard pair (Z2W2), double-hover pincer (Z3W3), double-choir (Z5W1) — but they are density beats, not idea beats.
- The tail is the problem: Zone 5 stacks choir+guard+hover+charger+lecturer across all three waves, and on Nightmare this is one of the two documented walls (F3). Choirmaster as a stationary volley turret + add top-ups makes the longest boss also the least mobile. Verdict: **Adventure = padded but clearable; Nightmare = unfair at Zone 5.** The `kidnap` cut at load is the campaign's only mid-run story beat and it lands before Zone 1, leaving ~3 min of pure loop after it.

### Stage 3 — CLAUDIA'S MOONLIT KEEP (Wingmen 360+360+200×2): **Longest and best-structured; padded middle, pressured tail**
- Bot: 211.3 / 209.4 / 219.8 s; 14 hits / 3 deaths solo-stage — the designed difficulty peak, and the death count proves respawn-with-progress is load-bearing here. Boss fight ~49.3 s. 56 wave enemies; pace ×1.15; final wave is 6 enemies staggered over 4 s.
- The Wingmen finale is the campaign's strongest pacing object: band-volley phase, teleport-smash phase (presentation flaw F13, rules fine), split into two 1-HP-floored targets whose *only* kill is the Papacito finisher, with an 8 s shake-trio drip preventing the softlock. That is a real RWB-shape climax and the check `PAPACITO FINISHER READY` + HUD prompt teaches it in situ. Verdict: **finale = OK-to-good; Zones 3–5 = padding with rising unfairness on Nightmare (second documented wall).** Claudia caged in the boss arena is the one environmental storytelling beat in any stage — more of this, earlier, would do pacing work that HP cannot.

Cross-stage rhythm note: boredom risk is front-loaded in each stage's Zones 4 (post-lesson repetition), panic risk is back-loaded in Zone 5 Wave 2–3 + boss Phase III adds. There is no authored valley anywhere: wave sizes go 3→3→4→4→5 with no breather, and full heals (F8) erase the consequence curve that would otherwise create one organically.

## 6. What already feels good (keep exactly this)

1. **Poise.** Flinch at most every 0.45 s (0.9 s heavies), bonks always stagger ([yettis.js:25](../src/yettis.js:25)). Packs close in under a held stream instead of stun-locking. This is the rebuild's best single feel change and any hit-stop retune (F2) must preserve it.
2. **Dad's impact-frame resolve.** The wipe fires when `age >= impact` (enter + frame 6 of the swat, [src/dad.js:33](../src/dad.js:33)), driven by the sim clock so it holds through pauses, with catchphrase, flash, shake, and star spray on `dadhit`. Cause and effect line up — the standard Papacito (F12) should be brought to.
3. **Boss gates.** One long bar, 66%/33% gates, 1.1 s untouchable roar, hostile clear, Phase-II shake drip, per-phase adds and banners. RWB-shaped and readable. It needs punctuation (F13), not redesign.
4. **Telegraph windows.** Charger 0.72/pace, boss tells 0.5–0.65/pace, one guaranteed safe band on every volley, rush overshoot through Bob's marked spot so the jump lesson is honest. Fair bones; only the rendering is monotone (F10).
5. **Bob's hurt ladder.** Hit → 0.28 s hurt; 3rd hit in 2 s or any charger hit → knockdown → getup with 1.9 s i-frames; dash with 0.28 s i-frames on ground and air. Good phone forgiveness curve.
6. **Wave plumbing.** `[type, side, delay]` staggering with flanks, plus the empty-arena 0.5 s pull-in, eliminated both the old whole-list dumps and dead air ([campaign.js:79](../src/campaign.js:79)).
7. **Voice life.** Barks are wired with sane cooldowns (Where-is-Claudia 18 s, Cory joke rare, Yetti cycle 7 s — [src/barks.js](../src/barks.js)) and the locked lines sit in rotation without spamming. Dad's D1–D3 shuffle bag avoids immediate repeats across sessions ([src/dad-call-cutscene.js:19](../src/dad-call-cutscene.js:19)).
8. **Left-third camera discipline.** `CAM_LEAD 0.28`, free-roam cap at 1/3, lock-in easing with snap only on respawn/teleport ([src/camera.js](../src/camera.js)). It honors the lock and reads as "world scrolls to Bob," as intended. (It is also shake-free by accident — F6 — so adding trauma must go *through* this, not around it.)
